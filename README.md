## RaveRunner
Game made for jumpstart haven
A gravity-flipping runner through a neon rave. Dodge and parry discs fired by enemy bots as you race to claim the Magic Rave Horn.
</div>

## Inputs

Press E to parry

Press W or S to change gravity
